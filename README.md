# Hersafe-
HerSafe GBV Awareness and Support Skill

About HerSafe

HerSafe is a gender-based violence (GBV) awareness, prevention, and support project designed to help women and girls understand gender-based violence, recognize warning signs, learn about their rights, and explore safe ways to seek help.

The project focuses on both rural and urban communities, especially people who have limited access to smartphones, internet services, or formal support systems. It promotes accessible information, community awareness, and responsible connections to appropriate support services.

Purpose of the Skill

This skill guides AI agents in creating useful, respectful, and survivor-centred GBV resources. It supports the development of awareness materials, community programmes, project proposals, research documents, referral guides, and practical solutions for improving access to support.

Key Functions

- Awareness and education: Explain different forms of GBV, including physical, emotional, sexual, and economic abuse, as well as harmful practices.
- Prevention: Develop educational campaigns, school and community workshops, and safety-awareness materials.
- Survivor-centred support: Provide compassionate, non-judgmental information without blaming survivors or pressuring them to disclose personal experiences.
- Safe referrals: Help identify relevant shelters, healthcare providers, legal assistance, counselling services, and protection organizations. Contact details and services must be verified before being presented as confirmed.
- Rural accessibility: Recommend community outreach, trusted local organizations, printed materials, trained community volunteers, and other options for people without smartphones or reliable internet.
- Research and documentation: Assist with proposals, research methods, project reports, needs assessments, and ethical data collection.
- Project development: Help plan HerSafe features, implementation steps, partnerships, monitoring, and evaluation.

Safety and Ethical Principles

HerSafe must protect privacy, respect personal choices, avoid harmful assumptions, and prioritize the immediate safety of people seeking help. It must not invent statistics, support contacts, laws, or service availability. Sensitive information should be collected only when necessary and handled responsibly.

HerSafe is an awareness and referral-support project, not a replacement for emergency services, qualified medical care, professional counselling, or legal advice.
